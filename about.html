PT. Robust Celebes Indonesia is a company in the field of Multimodal Logistics Service, Sea, Air, Land freight forwarding, warehouse management, Stevedoring and the movement of bulk material mining, Oil & Gas Industry.

Our core is handling project, Breakbulk and Heavylift cargo, Mining, Offshore, Project cargo, Vessel Agency, Custom Clearance, Domestics and International Freight Forwarding and Marine Logistics Services.

Our Vision

To be the leading and most trusted global multimodal logistics service provider in Indonesia, recognized for delivering excellence, driving operational efficiency, and creating sustainable value for our clients, partners, and the communities we serve.

Our Mission

To seamlessly connect businesses across the globe through integrated transportation solutions. We prioritize sustainability, safety, and continuous improvement in providing multimodal operations. We aim to optimize logistics, enhance domestic connectivity especially East of Indonesia and foster economic growth while prioritizing safety, and the highest standards of service excellence. Our mission is to handle every shipment with care, passion, and modern supply chain solutions, empowering people and shaping the future of logistics in the region and beyond.
